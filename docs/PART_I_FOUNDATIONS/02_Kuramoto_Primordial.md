## Mathematical Foundation

### The Kuramoto Model

For $N$ coupled oscillators:

$$\frac{d\theta_i}{dt} = \omega_i + \frac{K}{N}\sum_{j=1}^N \sin(\theta_j - \theta_i)$$

**Where:**
- $\theta_i(t)$ = phase of oscillator $i$
- $\omega_i$ = natural frequency (intrinsic)
- $K$ = coupling strength
- $N$ = number of oscillators

### Order Parameter

Synchronization degree measured by:

$$r e^{i\psi} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{j=1}^N e^{i\theta_i}$$

**Interpretation:**
- $r = 0$ → Complete desynchronization (incoherent)
- $0 < r < 1$ → Partial synchronization
- $r = 1$ → Perfect synchronization (coherent)

### Critical Transition

Synchronization emerges at critical coupling:

$$K > K_c = \frac{2}{\pi g(0)}$$

Where $g(\omega)$ is frequency distribution.

**Below $K_c$:** Incoherent (each oscillator independent)

**Above $K_c$:** Synchronized fraction emerges:

$$r \approx \sqrt{1 - K_c/K}$$

**Phase transition!** Second-order in thermodynamic sense.

## What is Geometric Frustration?

### Classical Definition

**From condensed matter physics:**

A system is **geometrically frustrated** when its local energy-minimizing configuration is incompatible with the global geometry or topology of the space it occupies.

**Classic example:** Antiferromagnetic spins on triangular lattice

```
    ↑
   / \
  ↓   ?
```

- Spin 1: ↑
- Spin 2: ↓ (antiparallel to 1)
- Spin 3: ??? (can't be antiparallel to BOTH 1 and 2!)

**Result:** Frustration!

### General Formulation

For system with interaction energy $H$:

$$H = \sum_{\langle ij \rangle} J_{ij} \mathbf{S}_i \cdot \mathbf{S}_j$$

**Frustrated when:**

$$\min_{\{\mathbf{S}_i\}} H \neq \sum_{\langle ij \rangle} \min J_{ij} \mathbf{S}_i \cdot \mathbf{S}_j$$

Cannot minimize all terms simultaneously!

**Consequence:** Macroscopic degeneracy of ground state

$$\Omega_0 \sim e^{S_0 N}$$

Where $S_0$ = residual entropy per site, $N$ = system size

**This is HUGE:** Even at T = 0, entropy $\neq$ 0!
